LoanLens is an independent educational resource. You may use the public information and calculators for **personal planning, learning and comparison**. No account or payment is required to use the publicly available tools.

## Estimates are not official decisions

Results are based on the numbers you enter and on documented assumptions. LoanLens does not hold, service, disburse or change loans. An estimate is not a quote, an approval, a legal determination or a guarantee of forgiveness.

For an account-specific decision, compare your results with [Federal Student Aid](https://studentaid.gov/), the actual loan servicer or the relevant college financial aid office. Review the [calculator disclaimer](/disclaimer/) and [methodology and sources](/sources/) before relying on a figure.

## Information you provide

Do not enter account passwords, Social Security numbers or other identifying documents into the calculators. If you share a scenario URL, anyone with that URL may see the scenario values included in it. See the [privacy policy](/privacy/) for details.

## Links to other sites

Links to Federal Student Aid, GitHub and other sites are provided for context. Those services have their own terms and privacy practices. LoanLens does not control their websites or decisions.

## Errors and feedback

If you find a bug, outdated reference or accessibility problem, you can file a report using the [public contact instructions](/contact/). Do not include personal or confidential borrower records in a public report.

These explanations describe the service's intended use; they do not create an advisory relationship between you and LoanLens.
