## Repayment questions

### Why did I get a SAVE notice?

Your servicer is giving you a window to select a different eligible plan. Check the <EditorialLink slug="save-ended-what-now">SAVE transition guide</EditorialLink> for both notice windows and what happens when they expire.

### Can I use both RAP and IBR?

Some borrowers with older federal Direct Loans can choose between them. The <EditorialLink slug="rap-vs-ibr">RAP and IBR comparison</EditorialLink> explains how a new loan can change eligibility.

### Why does my monthly RAP bill change with dependents?

RAP reduces the amount for the dependents claimed on the tax return used in the calculation. The [income tool](/rap-payment-by-income/) shows the effect within each income band.

### Which dependents do I enter if I am married?

Enter the joint-return count when comparing joint filing, and only your own claimed dependents for separate filing. Compare both in the [married RAP tool](/tools/married-rap-impact/).

### Can RAP payments count for PSLF?

They can count when you meet the loan, employment and payment requirements. Use the <EditorialLink slug="rap-and-pslf">RAP and PSLF guide</EditorialLink> to check which record to use.

### Can Parent PLUS borrowers move to IBR?

A limited route may apply to older Direct Consolidation Loans that repaid Parent PLUS. The [Parent PLUS checker](/tools/parent-plus-consolidation-checker/) identifies which dates and payment records to find.

### Does a Tiered Standard term change as I pay down the loan?

The tier is based on the Direct Loan amount when repayment begins. The <EditorialLink slug="tiered-standard">Tiered Standard guide</EditorialLink> explains how to compare the tier with time left on a current schedule.

### Is the projected forgiveness amount the tax I might owe?

The number in the estimate is the balance the plan could discharge. The <EditorialLink slug="idr-forgiveness-taxes">tax guide</EditorialLink> explains why the tax effect depends on the type and year of discharge.
