## Terms used on LoanLens

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Adjusted gross income (AGI) | Income after certain adjustments as reported on a federal tax return. |
| Borrower | The person legally responsible for repaying a loan. |
| Capitalization | Adding unpaid interest to the loan principal. |
| Direct Consolidation Loan | A federal loan used to combine eligible federal education loans. |
| Disbursement | The date loan funds are paid out. |
| Discretionary income | For IBR, income above the applicable poverty allowance. |
| Forgiveness | Cancellation of an eligible remaining balance after program conditions are met. |
| Income-Based Repayment (IBR) | A federal plan that bases eligible payments on discretionary income. |
| Income-Contingent Repayment (ICR) | An income-driven plan relevant to some existing Parent PLUS consolidation loans. |
| Income-driven repayment (IDR) | A category of plans that calculate payments using income and the applicable household rules. |
| Parent PLUS | A federal loan taken out by an eligible parent to help pay a student’s education costs. |
| Poverty guideline | A federal income table that varies by year, region and household size. |
| Principal | The amount borrowed that remains unpaid, plus any interest that has been added to it. |
| Public Service Loan Forgiveness (PSLF) | Federal forgiveness of eligible Direct Loan balances after qualifying payments and employment. |
| Repayment Assistance Plan (RAP) | An income-driven federal plan using AGI and claimed dependents to calculate payments. |
| SAVE | A discontinued federal income-driven repayment plan. |
| Servicer | The company assigned to handle the loan’s bill, account records and repayment requests. |
| Tiered Standard | A fixed-payment plan with a term based on the amount of Direct Loans at repayment entry. |
