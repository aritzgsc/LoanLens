LoanLens is an independent planning tool. Its rules and examples should be checked against primary publications, especially when loan disbursement dates or repayment rules are important to your decision.

## Federal repayment plans

- [Federal Student Aid: Repayment Plans](https://studentaid.gov/manage-loans/repayment/plans) — official plan descriptions and qualification considerations.
- [Federal Student Aid: Top FAQs on Income-Driven Repayment](https://studentaid.gov/articles/faqs-idr-plan/) — loan types, dates and RAP/IBR considerations.
- [Federal Student Aid: Repayment Calculator](https://studentaid.gov/articles/repayment-calculator/) — official comparison resource.
- [Federal Student Aid: SAVE borrower transition](https://studentaid.gov/articles/save/) — current SAVE transition guidance.
- [2026 U.S. Department of Education repayment regulations](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-05-01/html/2026-08556.htm) — regulatory details used in the model.

## Forgiveness, income and other costs

- [Federal Student Aid: Public Service Loan Forgiveness](https://studentaid.gov/manage-loans/forgiveness-cancellation/public-service) — program requirements and tracking.
- [U.S. Department of Health and Human Services: Poverty Guidelines](https://aspe.hhs.gov/poverty-guidelines/) — reference tables for income-driven plan calculations.
- [Federal Student Aid: Evaluating Financial Aid Offers](https://studentaid.gov/articles/evaluating-financial-aid-offers/) — cost of attendance, aid and net-price considerations.
- [Internal Revenue Service: Publication 4681](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p4681) — cancellation of debt and potential tax implications.

## Inspect the inputs and assumptions

The public repository contains the [rule values and their verification status](https://github.com/aritzgsc/LoanLens/blob/main/src/data/rules.json), [data-source inventory](https://github.com/aritzgsc/LoanLens/blob/main/docs/DATA_SOURCES.md) and [projection assumptions](https://github.com/aritzgsc/LoanLens/blob/main/docs/ASSUMPTIONS.md). Some operational details and future changes may remain **unverified**.

**An official source citation does not make a LoanLens calculation an official account quote.** Confirm account-specific requirements with your servicer.
