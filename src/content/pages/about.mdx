LoanLens is an **independent educational website** offering free tools and plain-language guides about United States student loans and college financial aid. It is a small project published under the LoanLens name, **not a bank, lender, loan servicer, school or government agency**. It does not claim to be a registered company.

## What LoanLens does

The [student loan calculator](/calculator/) produces model-based repayment estimates from the figures you enter. The [college aid comparison tool](/tools/compare-college-aid-offers/) helps separate net price, loans and the amount left to cover. The [guides](/guides/) explain terms and trade-offs with links to official sources.

Results are **estimates, not official eligibility decisions**. Federal program rules, lender records and your own circumstances can change the outcome. Verify any decision using [Federal Student Aid](https://studentaid.gov/), your loan servicer or the relevant college financial aid office.

## How to check our work

The public [source code](https://github.com/aritzgsc/LoanLens) includes calculation logic, automated tests, assumptions and a [documented data-source inventory](https://github.com/aritzgsc/LoanLens/blob/main/docs/DATA_SOURCES.md). Automated testing helps catch mistakes, but it is not a substitute for official repayment records or independent legal review.

See the [editorial policy](/editorial-policy/), [sources](/sources/), [disclaimer](/disclaimer/) and [advertising disclosure](/advertising/) for more details.

## Corrections and feedback

Found a calculation error, broken link or unclear explanation? Use our [public GitHub issue tracker](https://github.com/aritzgsc/LoanLens/issues/new). Issues are visible to everyone and generally require a GitHub account. **Do not post student loan account numbers, tax returns, identity documents or private financial details.**
