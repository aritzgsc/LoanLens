LoanLens is an independent educational site for U.S. student loan borrowers. The calculator compares available repayment paths using published rules and the figures you enter. The guides explain where projected and actual bills can diverge.

## Who determines your payment

Your servicer administers your bill and federal program eligibility depends on your account and program requirements. Use our estimates to frame questions and verify key information with Federal Student Aid and your servicer. LoanLens has no affiliation with the U.S. Department of Education or any loan servicer.
