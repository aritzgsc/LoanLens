LoanLens is a free, independent educational website. Advertising may help support its hosting and maintenance, but an advertisement does **not** make a repayment plan, college or loan product the right choice for you.

## Ads are separate from the calculations

The loan calculators use the values you enter, documented assumptions and the project's publicly available calculation code. Ad placements are not inputs to repayment formulas and do not change a modeled loan balance, annual cost, qualifying-payment count or projected payment.

Advertising can be labeled separately from editorial content and interactive results. Do not mistake an advertisement for a Federal Student Aid recommendation, an eligibility decision or an official financial aid offer.

## Commercial links and outside providers

The website may link to another provider's website for convenience or as a commercial referral. Such links, if enabled, should be clearly identified. LoanLens does not guarantee the accuracy, suitability, availability or terms of a third-party product. Always read the provider's own terms and disclosures.

## Privacy and advertising choices

If advertising is enabled, third-party services such as Google may process device and usage information to serve or measure ads, subject to applicable privacy controls. Review the [LoanLens privacy policy](/privacy/) and [Google's information about data from partner sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

An **Advertising privacy choices** control will appear in the footer when the site's compatible consent system is configured and active. If you do not see it, do not assume that a specific consent choice has been saved.

## Independence and corrections

To report an advertisement presented as an official decision, a misleading commercial link or another site issue, follow the [Contact instructions](/contact/). Public GitHub issues are not a confidential support channel.

For our approach to sources, model verification and corrections, see the [editorial policy](/editorial-policy/) and [sources](/sources/).
