Ad placements are separate from calculator inputs and results. Ads and partner offers are labeled as advertising. Google AdSense may serve ads if enabled and allowed by your privacy choices. The advertising tools may use cookies or similar signals. You can review the privacy policy when it is published alongside this disclosure.

## Affiliate offers

A sponsored link may compensate LoanLens when someone uses it. An offer’s placement or compensation does not determine plan eligibility, projected payments or comparison badges. **Refinancing federal loans into private loans can permanently remove access to federal repayment protections and forgiveness.** Confirm terms and the effect on your existing loans before considering refinancing.
