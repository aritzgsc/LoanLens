LoanLens may show paid ads or optional commercial links. Ads are separate from the repayment calculation. A paid link or advertisement does not establish that a product suits your loans. Review a provider’s own terms before using an offer.

When available, use the advertising privacy choices link in the footer to manage permissions. See the <EditorialLink slug="privacy">Privacy policy</EditorialLink> for how advertising and shared calculator links work.
