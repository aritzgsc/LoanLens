We want LoanLens's loan calculators, guides and results to be usable on phones, laptops, with keyboards and with assistive technology. **If something is difficult to operate or understand, please report it.** We cannot guarantee perfect compatibility with every device or assistive tool.

## Reading and navigating the site

The calculator uses visible labels and explanatory hints to identify inputs. You can move between most interactive controls with the **Tab** key and use **Enter** or **Space** to activate buttons. Charts are accompanied by text-based result information where available; do not rely on color alone to interpret a financial result.

Some financial tables are wider than mobile screens. Where needed, their containers scroll horizontally; keyboard users can focus those regions before scrolling.

## If something does not work

[Open a public accessibility issue](https://github.com/aritzgsc/LoanLens/issues/new) with:

- The page URL and the control or information that was difficult to use.
- Your browser, device size, and assistive technology if relevant.
- The steps you followed and the result you expected.
- An example using **invented** loan values, if a calculation is involved.

GitHub issues are public. **Never include your real loan account, contact details, disability or health information, private documents or credentials** in a report.

We review reported barriers through the public repository and can update the site when an issue is confirmed. We do not offer a private support channel for sensitive requests.

## Alternative ways to understand a result

Try the [repayment comparison guide](/guides/compare-student-loan-repayment/) for a text explanation, the [Tools directory](/tools/) to choose a different calculator, or the [Sources page](/sources/) to check an official statement. Contact the loan servicer for account-specific assistance.

This page describes our approach to accessibility; it is **not** a certification of compliance with a particular accessibility standard.
