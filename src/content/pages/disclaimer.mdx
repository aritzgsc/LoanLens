LoanLens is an independent educational site. It is not affiliated with the U.S. Department of Education. Its material and modeled results are estimates for planning and do not provide financial, tax or legal advice.

The model uses the loan information and assumptions you enter. Actual eligibility, payments, interest, credits, forgiveness and taxes can differ based on your repayment history, the servicing record and rules that apply to you. LoanLens cannot enroll you in a plan, certify your payments or approve forgiveness.

Use the [Federal Student Aid Loan Simulator](https://studentaid.gov/loan-simulator/) and verify account-specific information with your servicer before changing plans. Consult the relevant tax authority or a qualified professional for questions about your circumstances.
