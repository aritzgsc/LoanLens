LoanLens is an **independent educational website** and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the U.S. Department of Education, a financial institution, a loan servicer or a college.

The calculators produce illustrative planning estimates from information you choose to enter. They **do not** determine eligibility for a federal plan, guarantee a monthly payment, confirm a qualifying payment history, enroll you in a repayment program, or approve forgiveness.

## Why estimates can differ

Your actual amount can depend on loan disbursement dates, exact interest accrual, outstanding interest, loan servicer allocation rules, employment history, income certification, plan transitions, consolidation, changing family circumstances, tax consequences and rules updated after the model's review date. The model uses documented simplifications, including estimated monthly rather than daily interest.

Forgiveness is not guaranteed. The amount labeled as estimated forgiveness may have separate tax consequences.

## What to do before making a decision

Compare LoanLens results with the [official Federal Student Aid Repayment Calculator](https://studentaid.gov/articles/repayment-calculator/), review your actual loan information on [StudentAid.gov](https://studentaid.gov/), and confirm eligibility, deadlines and payment details with your servicer. Ask a qualified adviser about individual financial, legal or tax circumstances.

Do not share student aid credentials, tax returns or other private account data through public links or GitHub issues. See the [privacy policy](/privacy/) and [editorial methodology](/editorial-policy/).
