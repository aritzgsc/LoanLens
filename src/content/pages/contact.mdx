LoanLens accepts **public technical feedback and correction reports** through the [GitHub issue tracker](https://github.com/aritzgsc/LoanLens/issues/new). It is suitable for reporting an inaccurate calculation, broken source, confusing explanation or accessibility issue.

## Choose what to report

Every option opens a **public GitHub issue**. Pick the closest category; you do not need to provide any private student loan or account information.

## What to include

- The URL of the LoanLens page with the issue
- What appears incorrect and what you expected instead
- A **hypothetical** example to reproduce a calculation issue
- A link to an official source, if you know one

## Please protect your privacy

**GitHub issues are public** and posting generally requires a GitHub account. Do not publish your real loan balances, Social Security number, bank details, login credentials, tax returns or any other sensitive information. LoanLens does not provide private borrower assistance through GitHub.

If you need help with an actual federal student loan account, use [StudentAid.gov](https://studentaid.gov/) or contact your loan servicer. If your request contains personal or confidential information, do **not** use the public issue tracker. A dedicated private contact channel is not currently offered by this site.
