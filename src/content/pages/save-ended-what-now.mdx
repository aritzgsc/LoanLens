## Who this applies to

Use this page if you were enrolled in SAVE or had a SAVE application pending. A pending application can lead to a different outcome from an active SAVE enrollment. Federal Student Aid explains which borrowers in school or in a grace period need no immediate action.

## What to check

### Find the deadline in the notice

Open the message from your servicer and note the date it was sent. The first notice gives affected borrowers {rules.saveNoticeDays.value} days to choose an eligible plan. If you receive a final notice, it starts a separate {rules.saveFinalNoticeDays.value}-day window. Enter the notice date and type in the [deadline tool](/deadlines/) and use your servicer’s stated date if the two disagree.

| Your status | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| First notice received | Read the first deadline and look for any pending plan application. |
| Final notice received | Confirm the replacement plan and when the first bill is due. |
| Deadline passed | Open your current repayment schedule and check which plan your servicer assigned. |
| SAVE application pending | Check whether you were returned to the plan you held before applying. |

### Check the bill if you take no action

A borrower who was enrolled in SAVE and misses the final deadline can go onto Standard or Tiered Standard. The relevant loan dates determine which fixed plan applies. Under a fixed plan, changes in income do not automatically change the scheduled payment. Open the first statement after the switch, compare the due date and amount with your previous statement, and check that any scheduled bank withdrawal uses the new amount.

You can still ask the servicer about switching to an eligible plan after a fixed plan assignment. A submitted request and an accepted request are different account events. Look for the effective date and read the bill while the request is pending.

### Compare the plans your loans can use

For student Direct Loans, check whether the portfolio qualifies for both RAP and IBR. New Direct borrowing on or after {rules.newLoanDate.value} can change which options remain. A consolidation loan containing parents’ loans needs a separate eligibility check. Use the [Parent PLUS tool](/tools/parent-plus-consolidation-checker/) before interpreting the comparison for that portfolio.

PAYE and ICR have a separate transition tied to {rules.sunsetDate.value}. That date does not replace the deadline written in an individual SAVE notice. If you are pursuing PSLF, compare eligible replacement plans using each loan’s certified count. Ask about the exact SAVE months in your record. A general description of forbearance cannot establish what will count on your account.

## Common mistakes

- Using the PAYE and ICR transition date as the deadline from a SAVE notice.
- Assuming a submitted replacement request changed the next automatic debit.
- Counting every SAVE forbearance month as PSLF or IDR credit.
