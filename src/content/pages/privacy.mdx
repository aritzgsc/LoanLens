## Calculator inputs and scenario links

The calculator processes the inputs you enter in your browser. If you copy a scenario link, the link contains those inputs. Anyone you share the link with can read them. Leave account identifiers and other personal information out of a shared scenario.

## Anonymous traffic measurement

LoanLens uses [Vercel Web Analytics](https://vercel.com/docs/analytics/privacy-policy) to understand aggregate visitors, page views and referral sources. Vercel describes this measurement as **cookie-free**, with visitor activity aggregated rather than linked to identifiable user accounts. It may process the page path, approximate country, referrer, device/browser category and visit time. Analytics does **not** require a LoanLens account.

The loan calculator's financial entries stay in your browser; **they are not sent as analytics events**. Please avoid inserting personal information in URLs or sharing scenario links containing private financial details. You may use privacy-focused browser controls or content blockers, which can affect whether a visit is counted.

## Hosting and advertising

The hosting provider may process technical information to deliver the site. If advertising is enabled, Google Privacy & messaging can process your privacy choices. When ads are served, Google and other advertising providers may place or read browser cookies and use web beacons, IP addresses, or other identifiers to deliver and measure ads. Review [how Google uses data from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). Use **Advertising privacy choices** in the footer when that control is available.

## Public issue reports and private information

The <EditorialLink slug="contact">Contact page</EditorialLink> explains the site's **public** GitHub issue tracker for reporting errors. Issues can be read by other people, and the service hosting the issue tracker has its own privacy practices. Do **not** post personal information or sensitive borrower details in an issue.

LoanLens does not currently provide a private email or confidential account-support channel. Before launching advertising or accepting private requests, the publisher needs to review applicable privacy transparency obligations and make appropriate contact information available.
