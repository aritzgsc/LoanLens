## Calculator inputs and scenario links

The calculator processes the inputs you enter in your browser. If you copy a scenario link, the link contains those inputs. Anyone you share the link with can read them. Leave account identifiers and other personal information out of a shared scenario.

## Hosting and advertising

The hosting provider may process technical information to deliver the site. If advertising is configured, the consent provider can process your privacy choices. When advertising is permitted, the advertising provider can receive browser and device information. Use the advertising privacy choices control when it is available.

To report a privacy concern or ask about a message you sent, use <EditorialLink slug="contact">Contact</EditorialLink>.
