## Calculator inputs and shared links

Calculations run on your device. LoanLens has no account or database for calculator inputs and does not send the balance, income, or other values you enter to a LoanLens server. The site uses ordinary web hosting, and the host may process data needed to serve and secure requests.

If you choose to copy a scenario link, its fragment encodes the inputs. The fragment is not sent as part of an HTTP request, but anyone with the complete link can read it. Your browser, clipboard, or applications where you paste the link may store it. The site reads the fragment locally to reconstruct the scenario.

## Analytics and advertising

When enabled, Cloudflare Web Analytics measures aggregate visits and performance without using cookies for tracking. Google AdSense may use cookies, device information, or similar technologies to deliver, measure and personalize advertising with applicable choices. Google Privacy & messaging displays consent and state opt-out controls where available. Use Advertising privacy choices in the site footer once the consent service is enabled. The advertising gate also honors a Global Privacy Control opt-out signal.

See [how Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and [Google’s privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
