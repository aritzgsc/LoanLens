## Evidence and estimates

LoanLens cites federal student aid materials, regulations and other primary sources for repayment rules. The <EditorialLink slug="sources">sources page</EditorialLink> lists the references used in the comparison. A calculator result can include a flagged assumption when a published rule or the available inputs cannot establish an account-specific outcome.

## Corrections

Report a factual error through <EditorialLink slug="contact">Contact</EditorialLink>. Include a link to the page, the sentence or result you think is wrong, and the source if you have it. The editorial status describes the page; it does not certify a borrower’s eligibility.
