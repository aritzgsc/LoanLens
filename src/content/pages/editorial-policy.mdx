## Purpose and independence

LoanLens publishes **educational, model-based** student loan tools and guides, not individualized financial, legal or tax advice. It is not affiliated with the U.S. Department of Education. Advertisements, when enabled, are separate from the model's outputs and do not decide which result is displayed.

## Sources and verification

Published repayment parameters are recorded in the [open-source rules inventory](https://github.com/aritzgsc/LoanLens/blob/main/src/data/rules.json) with source links, effective dates, retrieval dates and verification labels. Our [source list](/sources/) points to Federal Student Aid, federal regulations and relevant agencies.

When rules or servicing practices are unclear, the calculator can show **unverified assumption warnings** or decline to produce an account-specific comparison. A source label of *verified* means that the stated rule was cross-checked against a referenced publication; it does not guarantee the rule applies to every borrower.

## Methodology and testing

The calculator uses estimated monthly interest and payments, rather than connecting to your account. The [documented modeling assumptions](https://github.com/aritzgsc/LoanLens/blob/main/docs/ASSUMPTIONS.md) explain how estimates can differ from servicing records. Automated tests, link checks and browser audits run when code changes are proposed. These tests help catch regressions but cannot verify every personal borrowing situation.

## Corrections

If you spot an error, [open a public correction report](https://github.com/aritzgsc/LoanLens/issues/new) with a page link, an explanation and an official source if possible. **Only use made-up example numbers**, never your own identifying or sensitive records.

We aim to correct confirmed errors in the source code or guide and retain the change history in the public repository. We cannot promise a response time or official repayment guidance.

## Updates and limitations

Guides show update dates. A publication date or automated test success does not mean a future regulatory change has been incorporated. Verify time-sensitive decisions with [Federal Student Aid's current repayment plans](https://studentaid.gov/manage-loans/repayment/plans).
