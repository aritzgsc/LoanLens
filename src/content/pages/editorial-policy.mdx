Each article is an editorial draft until a human checks its primary sources, worked examples and applicability notes. A published article shows when it was reviewed and when the underlying text was last checked. We use statutes, final regulations and agency guidance, and mark material uncertainty when sources or operational procedures leave questions open.

## How the model works

The model forecasts scheduled payments with a monthly interest approximation, a defined projection start and annual income changes. It treats dollar totals as undiscounted and excludes forgiveness taxes. A servicer calculates actual bills using its own account records, applicable interest periods and confirmed credit. Portfolio-level payment allocations and future assumptions can cause differences.
