Public Service Loan Forgiveness (PSLF) involves more than reaching a number on a calculator. The official process considers your **loan type**, **qualifying employer**, **repayment history**, and the number of payments that count toward the program.

A planning tracker can help you visualize the remaining months. It cannot determine whether a month qualifies or whether your application will be approved.

## Start with the official count—not your own estimate

Open [StudentAid.gov](https://studentaid.gov/) and check your PSLF progress. Federal Student Aid explains how to view counts and how employment certification affects the number of recognized qualifying payments.

A loan can have a different qualifying count from another loan. Do not assume one count automatically applies to an entire portfolio, especially if loans have different histories.

Read [How to Manage Your PSLF Progress](https://studentaid.gov/articles/manage-your-pslf-progress/) for the official process.

## Worked example: what a tracker can show

Suppose your account shows **84 certified qualifying payments** for one loan. A simple projection would calculate:

**120 − 84 = 36 qualifying payments remaining.**

If your future employment, payment timing and other conditions all qualify, the target would be approximately 36 qualifying monthly payment periods away.

That is a **planning milestone**, not a guarantee. The actual count can be adjusted through review, certification or changes in your account, and not every future calendar month necessarily counts.

Use the [LoanLens PSLF payment tracker](/tools/pslf-payment-tracker/) to see a possible month from the **official count shown for one loan**.

## Why employment certification matters

Federal Student Aid reviews eligibility of the employer and relevant employment periods. You should check the official instructions for submitting a PSLF form and monitor the resulting payment count; simply working for a public or nonprofit employer does not by itself establish every requirement.

Use the official [PSLF Help Tool](https://studentaid.gov/pslf/) rather than assuming that a LoanLens scenario can certify employment.

## Questions if your count looks wrong

1. Am I looking at the correct **loan**?
2. Have I certified the relevant **employment periods**?
3. Are some months listed as **ineligible or not yet certified**?
4. Did a consolidation or another loan-status change affect the count?
5. Do I need to contact Federal Student Aid or the servicer for a record review?

Do not overwrite official values with guesses just to make the target date appear closer. Record what is shown, then follow up on discrepancies using the official process.

## What LoanLens does and does not verify

The tracker subtracts the number you enter from the commonly stated 120-payment goal and displays a projected target month. It assumes future months qualify. It does **not** import counts from your account, validate a past payment, determine qualifying employment or submit a PSLF application.

## Your next step

Check the official record, then open the [PSLF progress tracker](/tools/pslf-payment-tracker/) and enter the verified count for one loan. If you have multiple loans, repeat the calculation separately when their counts differ.
